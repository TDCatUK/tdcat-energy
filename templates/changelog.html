6 October 2026

A big overhaul: corrected maths throughout, new battery and gas data, and a security fix.

New

  • Batteries in the charts: the battery estimate now appears in Generation by Fuel Type, the Live Energy Flow and the 24-Hour Generation Mix, with one On/Off switch for all three. The flow's Storage node is now two-way (pumped storage plus batteries) and shows whether storage is charging or discharging overall.
  • Changelog: this page.
  • Gas storage compared with previous years: the Storage card compares today's stock with the same date over the previous five years and turns red, amber or green. It has a 12-month sparkline, and clicking it shows this year against every year back to 2020.
  • Frequency warning: the Frequency card changes colour when frequency is outside the 49.8–50.2 Hz operational limits.
  • National Gas Data: a new section with live linepack (gas held in the pipelines), total supply and demand, where gas is coming from (North Sea, Norway, LNG, storage, the Continent) and where it's going (homes and businesses, power stations, industry, exports, storage), a rough gas-for-power efficiency figure, and a 24-hour chart.
  • Grid Batteries (unofficial estimate): GB grid-scale battery charging and discharging, worked out every 5 minutes from Balancing Mechanism data, with a 24-hour chart. Batteries can also be picked in the single-fuel chart.

Fixed

  • Demand breakdown: rooftop solar and small wind were being counted twice, which made Transmission, National, Net and Gross demand about 6 GW too high around midday. The figures now match Elexon's own.
  • Generation mix: pumped storage showed as negative generation while pumping, and the charts rebuilt GW figures from percentages using different totals. The mix now adds up to exactly 100% and the charts use the real MW figures.
  • Battery level: now shows the same percentage as the Tesla app, which hides a 5% reserve.
  • Home "today" totals: now start at midnight UK time. During British Summer Time they were missing the first hour of the day.
  • Octopus daily figures: import, export and gas now cover one whole day, the same day for all three, with the right date. Previously they added up the latest 48 half-hours, which could span two days.
  • Octopus data: restored after the API key was renewed. There's a gap from 30 September 16:55 to 6 October 12:00 UTC.
  • Market index price: no longer drops to £0 just after midnight, and half-hours with no trades are ignored.
  • Carbon intensity: uses the forecast when the measured figure for the current half-hour isn't published yet, instead of showing 0.
  • Greenlink: the Ireland interconnector is now named properly and counted in the Ireland flow on the map.
  • Chart labels: the battery chart's axis no longer shows numbers like -0.2000000000000002.

Security

  • Settings: saving the dashboard settings now needs the admin login. Before this, anyone who found the address could change them.

Settings (Admin page)

  • Gas chart colours and the storage warning thresholds can be changed.
  • Batteries have their own colour, and new fuel colours are saved properly.
  • The admin page now tells you if a save fails, instead of always saying it worked.

About page

  • Rewritten and corrected: the four demand measures, frequency limits, imbalance and market prices, the dashed line on the generation chart, and UK changed to GB where it means the electricity grid.
  • New sections explaining the battery estimate, the gas data and the storage comparison.

Behind the scenes

  • The project is backed up to GitHub, with a history of every change.
  • The data collector's log was cleaned up and now keeps 90 days.
  • Old test scripts and database backups were removed.
