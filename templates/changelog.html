6 October 2026

A big overhaul: better maths, batteries and gas

New

  • I've added When Demand Shifts: close-ups of the morning, evening and night, when off-peak tariffs, sunset and cooking change demand.
  • I've added a duck curve chart showing how rooftop solar has hollowed out midday demand since 2010, with solar records that update daily.
  • I've added a National Gas section: gas in the pipelines, supply and demand, where it comes from and goes to, and a 24-hour chart.
  • Gas storage is now compared with the same date in previous years. Click the card for a year-by-year chart back to 2020.
  • I've added an unofficial Grid Batteries estimate with its own 24-hour chart.
  • Batteries now appear in the fuel mix, the 24-hour chart and the energy flow, where they have their own node. There's an On/Off switch if you'd rather leave the estimate out.
  • Click the Frequency card for every 15-second reading over the last hour, 6 hours or day, with today's low, high and time outside the normal limits. The card changes colour when it's outside them.
  • I've rewritten the About page and started this changelog.

Fixed

  • Demand was counting rooftop solar and small wind twice, making it about 6 GW too high at midday. It now matches Elexon's figures.
  • Pumped storage showed as negative generation when pumping. The fuel mix now adds up to 100%.
  • My home battery level now matches the Tesla app.
  • Today's home totals now start at midnight UK time.
  • Octopus daily figures now cover one whole day, with the right date. They were also missing for a week until I renewed the API key.
  • The market price no longer drops to £0 just after midnight.
  • Carbon intensity no longer shows 0 before the latest figure is published.
  • The Greenlink interconnector to Ireland now shows up properly.
